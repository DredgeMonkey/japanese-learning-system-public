📘 Log Study Session

⚠️ Timezone Notice: Google Apps Script saves dates in UTC.

If you're in a timezone behind UTC (like most US timezones), selecting today’s date may log it as the previous day.

➡️ To ensure accuracy:
– Pick the date one day ahead (e.g., choose April 30 to log April 29).
– Then run 🩺 Heal Session Anchors from the menu.

✅ Your dashboard will reflect the correct date automatically.
